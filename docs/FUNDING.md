## The ask, plainly

Fund the next falsifiable milestone. A small grant (microgrant scale) covers the researcher's time, an independent human methodological critique, the design and validation of the successor instrument, the infrastructure and public evidence needed to make it reproducible, and, only after the positive controls pass, the frontier or API compute for the channel test. The asymmetry favours the funder: a small amount now determines whether a larger intervention study is epistemically justified at all. We are not asking anyone to scale a scientifically demonstrated system; we are asking for the means to resolve one explicitly identified uncertainty.

## Why this is capital-efficient

The programme used inexpensive local-model diagnostics (three iterations, 112 episodes, hosted CI runners) to detect a measurement failure *before* spending on frontier evaluation, which had already been designed and costed. The evidence did not merely get recorded: it cancelled the planned expensive experiment. The same gating principle applies to future funding: expensive compute is unlocked only after lower-cost controls establish that the instrument can measure the intended construct. Cheap tests decide whether expensive experiments are epistemically justified.

## Kinds of resource

- **Money:** licensed data (F2), reviewer fees if institutional access is unavailable (F3), small infrastructure (F4).
- **Compute and infrastructure:** frontier/API compute for the successor channel test, gated on positive controls (F1); secondary environments (F4).
- **Institutional access and mentorship:** an independent human methodological critic for the successor protocol (F1); reviewers for attestations (F3). Access is not counted as funding received.
- **Time:** the binding constraint across F1, F4 and F5.

## Why trust this

The programme publishes what did not work: retractions, a contaminated baseline, an entire architecture removed, attestations that stopped re-validating, a seal left broken by decision, and now a behavioural measurement line it falsified and closed itself. Results that weakened the original research and funding thesis were preserved and used to change the programme. Hashes, counts, dates and limitations are verifiable without trusting private code. What is *not* claimed is equally explicit: no behavioural effect of CAIN, no safety, no frontier result, no external human validation yet.
