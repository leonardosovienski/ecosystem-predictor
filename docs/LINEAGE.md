## What the chronology does and does not show

- It shows the order *prediction → evaluation → reproducibility/governance → qualification → agent and its evaluation*.
- Research infrastructure appears twice: a first attempt (central aggregator) was invalidated and removed; the surviving form uses hash-pinned artefacts and file-based transport.
- The agent was started **before** formal qualification; the two developed in parallel, with qualification taking the agent as its integration target.
- **Interpretation, not fact:** that the agent's containment design *derives from* the evaluation failures above is our reading of the sequence. No dated document states that motivation explicitly.
- **Fact:** the October 2026 diagnostic line was closed and the frontier evaluation cancelled on the strength of its own negative results. **Interpretation:** this is the same pattern as the +44% ROI, the attack/defence model, the contaminated baseline, the LLM signal, the aggregator and the attestations: an apparent success converted into a narrower claim after adversarial evaluation. A recurring output of the programme is discovering when its own results are wrong, and acting on it before the next expensive step.
- Earlier projects (World Cup, esports, motorsport; June–July 2026) are the origin of the evaluation problems and of the temporal contract. They are outside the scope of this showcase and are mentioned only as prior work.
