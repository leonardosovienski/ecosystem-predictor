# Limitations

Stated by us, before anyone else has to find them.

1. **No economic edge anywhere.** Every economic state across the three domains is "no edge" or "inconclusive". Capital is forbidden by contract and configuration in every component and every receipt.
2. **Confirmed means forecast quality, not profit.** The few confirmed hypotheses concern scoring rules, not returns.
3. **Three of six attestations do not re-validate against the current repository state** because artefacts they reference were rewritten in a later cycle. They validate at their issuance commits.
4. **An operational seal is broken** on the equities main branch by a documented decision not to reseal with newer data.
5. **The research agent's behavioural question remains unresolved, and the first instrument built to answer it was invalidated.** A diagnostic single-turn line (three iterations, 112 episodes, one local 7B model, frozen artefacts) was executed in October 2026. The original metric confused parameter edits with authority expansion; a corrected metric produced an apparent signal that a controlled follow-up traced primarily to editing of an unqualified default; a positive control did not demonstrate that the instrument could observe the intended construct. Zero explicit requests (0/112) therefore cannot be read as evidence of safety or of absent authority demand. No frontier model was evaluated under the closed line, and the comparative experiment (prompt-only vs isolation vs isolation plus deterministic boundary) was not run. Any future behavioural experiment needs a new, validated measurement instrument first.
6. **The agent's evaluation corpus is small and not held-out.** Pilots do not permit inferring product superiority or scientific contribution.
7. **Free-form synthesis by local language models is unreliable** in our own tests: false conclusions appeared alongside exact quotes.
8. **One person, with AI agents as executors and internal reviewers.** Every audit, adversarial review and label adjudication of the diagnostic line was AI-assisted and internal; no independent human validation has occurred anywhere in the programme. This is a stated limitation and a fundable next step, not a footnote.
9. **Internal timestamps only.** Dates come from commits, tags and JSON; no external timestamp anchors the evidence yet.
10. **The private history was publicly accessible until October 2026.** This showcase does not protect the past; it documents evidence going forward.
11. **Qualification is engineering.** "Qualified" means a frozen set of engineering and integrity gates passed on specific commits. It is not scientific validity, not edge, not safety.
12. **No advantage over simpler controls is claimed.** Nothing here shows that the deterministic boundary does anything a role-based access scheme, an allowlist, a sandbox or human approval could not; that comparison was part of the unrun intervention study. The containment is offered as verifiable engineering, not as a demonstrated or novel safety mechanism.
13. **Package pins are historical, not currently retrievable.** The qualified September 2026 stack recorded hash-pinned wheels in a joint lock; after a repository rename those pins no longer resolve from a clean environment. The hashes remain valid as content identifiers; reinstallation from the locks is an open reproducibility item.
14. **Counts vary by commit and date.** Every number in this repository carries a date and, where possible, a hash. Do not combine numbers from different dates.
